Healthier
Beef & Broccoli

The takeout classic, rebuilt: lean flank steak velveted (not deep-fried), a light garlic-ginger sauce, and enough broccoli to make it a real plate.

The Order

谢谢
  • Flank steak, sliced thin against the grain1 lb
  • Baking soda (velveting)¼ tsp
  • Cornstarch (for beef)1 tbsp
  • Broccoli, cut into florets5 cups
  • Garlic, minced4 cloves
  • Fresh ginger, grated1 tbsp
  • Low-sodium soy sauce3 tbsp
  • Low-sodium broth½ cup
  • Honey1 tbsp
  • Rice vinegar1 tsp
  • Cornstarch (for sauce)1½ tsp
  • Toasted sesame oil1 tsp
  • Neutral oil (avocado/canola)1 tbsp

Swap in sirloin or skirt steak if that's what you've got — just slice thin, against the grain either way.

The Method

  1. Velvet the beef

    Toss the steak with baking soda and cornstarch. Rest 15 minutes — this is the trick that keeps takeout beef silky and tender, no deep-frying required.

  2. Mix the sauce

    Whisk soy sauce, broth, honey, rice vinegar, sauce cornstarch, and sesame oil until the cornstarch dissolves. Set aside.

  3. Steam the broccoli

    Steam or microwave the florets with a splash of water until bright green and just tender, about 3 minutes. Drain well.

  4. Sear the beef

    Heat the neutral oil in a wok over high heat. Spread the beef in one layer, sear undisturbed 1 minute, then stir-fry until just browned — about 2 minutes total. Remove.

  5. Aromatics

    Lower to medium. Add garlic and ginger; stir-fry 30 seconds until fragrant, not browned.

  6. Bring it together

    Pour in the sauce and simmer until glossy, about 1 minute. Return the beef and broccoli, toss to coat, and heat through. Serve over brown or cauliflower rice.
