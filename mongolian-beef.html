Mongolian Beef

Chuck steak, properly velveted and seared hard in tallow, glazed sweet and sticky with a fraction of the sugar and low-sodium soy. Rich beef, restaurant depth, a lighter tab.

The Order

谢谢
  • Beef chuck, sliced thin against the grain1 lb
The velvet
  • Baking soda½ tsp
  • Water1 tbsp
  • Low-sodium soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Cornstarch2 tbsp
Sear + glaze
  • Beef tallow (2 tbsp if deep-frying)1 tbsp
  • Green onions, in 2-inch pieces8 stalks
  • Garlic, minced4 cloves
  • Fresh ginger, grated1 tbsp
  • Low-sodium soy sauce¼ cup
  • Low-sodium broth or water⅓ cup
  • Brown sugar (¼ the usual)3 tbsp
  • Rice vinegar1 tsp
  • Red pepper flakes (optional)½ tsp
  • Toasted sesame oil1 tsp

Chuck is tougher and fattier than flank, so the baking-soda velvet really pays off here. Slice it thin against the grain while it's part-frozen for clean strips. Prefer flank? Drop the velvet rest to 15 min.

The Method

  1. Velvet the chuck · 30 min

    Toss the sliced chuck with the baking soda, water, and 1 tbsp soy. Rest 30 minutes — longer than flank needs, because chuck is a working muscle. Then rinse under cold water, squeeze dry, and toss with the cornstarch to a light, even coat. Rinsing is what tenderizes and also washes most of the added sodium away.

  2. Mix the glaze

    Stir together the ¼ cup soy, broth, brown sugar, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and pepper flakes until the sugar begins to dissolve. Set aside.

  3. Sear in tallow

    Melt the tallow in a wok over high heat until it just shimmers. Lay the beef in one layer and sear undisturbed 1 minute for a hard crust, then stir-fry until browned and just cooked, about 2 minutes. Remove to a plate. Deep-frying instead? Heat the tallow to 350°F, fry the coated beef in batches 2–3 min until crisp, and drain.

  4. Aromatics

    Pour off all but a thin film of tallow. Lower to medium, add garlic and ginger, and stir-fry 30 seconds until fragrant, not browned.

  5. Build the glaze

    Pour in the sauce and simmer 1–2 minutes until it reduces, darkens, and coats the back of a spoon.

  6. Finish with scallions

    Return the beef, add the green onions, and toss just until they wilt but stay bright — under a minute. Serve over brown or cauliflower rice.

Your fortune: Rinse the velvet, get the tallow smoking, sear in one layer — a tender chuck rewards a hot wok. Lucky numbers: 325, 24, 30.
