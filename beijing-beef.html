Beijing Beef

The crispy, sweet-and-tangy takeout favorite — velveted beef fried in tallow, bright bell peppers, and a ketchup-chili glaze with half the sugar of the drive-thru version.

Serves
4
Cook
25 min
Per plate
315 cal
Protein
26 g

The Order

谢谢
  • Flank steak, sliced thin against the grain1 lb
The velvet + crisp
  • Baking soda¼ tsp
  • Water1 tbsp
  • Low-sodium soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Cornstarch (coating)3 tbsp
  • Beef tallow, for frying3–4 tbsp
The vegetables
  • Red bell pepper, in 1-inch pieces1 large
  • Onion, in 1-inch pieces½ medium
Sweet-tangy glaze
  • Low-sodium soy sauce3 tbsp
  • Ketchup2 tbsp
  • Rice vinegar2 tbsp
  • Honey (½ the usual)2 tbsp
  • Chili garlic sauce (sambal)2 tsp
  • Garlic, minced4 cloves
  • Fresh ginger, grated1 tbsp
  • Low-sodium broth or water⅓ cup
  • Cornstarch (slurry)1 tsp
  • Toasted sesame oil1 tsp

Using chuck instead of flank? Give the velvet a full 30 min and rinse before coating — worth it for the tougher cut (adds ~60 cal/serving). Want it crispier for longer? Toss the fried beef in the glaze at the very last second, off heat.

The Method

  1. Velvet the beef · 15 min

    Toss the sliced flank with baking soda, water, and 1 tbsp soy. Rest 15 minutes (30 and a rinse if you're using chuck). Then coat evenly in the 3 tbsp cornstarch, shaking off the excess — a thin, dry coat is what crisps up.

  2. Mix the glaze

    Stir together the soy, ketchup, rice vinegar, honey, chili garlic sauce, broth, cornstarch slurry, and sesame oil until smooth. Set aside.

  3. Fry in tallow

    Melt the tallow in a wok over medium-high until it shimmers (about 350°F). Fry the beef in two batches so it stays crisp, 2–3 minutes each, until deep golden. Lift out and drain on a rack.

  4. Blister the peppers

    Pour off all but 1 tbsp tallow. Over high heat, stir-fry the bell pepper and onion 1–2 minutes — you want them hot and just-blistered but still crunchy.

  5. Aromatics + glaze

    Add the garlic and ginger; stir-fry 30 seconds. Pour in the glaze and simmer 1 minute until it turns glossy and clings to a spoon.

  6. Toss and serve

    Return the crispy beef, toss fast to coat, and pull it off the heat while the crust still has bite. Serve over brown or cauliflower rice.

Your fortune: Crisp in batches, glaze at the last second — the wok waits for no one. Lucky numbers: 315, 26, 12.
